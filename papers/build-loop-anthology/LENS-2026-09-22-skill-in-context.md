## Challenging his claims

**H1 — holds, with a sharper reason than he gave.** It is not only that C4 must prove it supports the
common way. The pass beside this one (Mentat's) found the loop reaches **no** seat outside c4 today —
three of three seeded projects, including card-game, whose own `model.md` copied the door and dropped
the loop. The skill is not a courtesy to others; it is how the loop reaches his own other projects.
*Falsifier:* open the shipped `SKILL.md` in a plain editor with no Claude running; if a person cannot
follow the loop from it alone, C4 has not proved it supports that way — it has only packaged itself.

**H2 — holds inside the shop, and must be re-said before it leaves.** "No parts are optional" is right
for whoever has chosen the loop: an à-la-carte loop is no loop (skip the final swing "just this once"
and the counter means nothing). But carried to a stranger as written, it becomes the thing his own
2026-08-09 note warns against — *"accidentally baking rules for other people's AI that restrict the
other users in ways they don't expect."* The reconciliation is already inside his own wording:
`qa-max-swings-default` is called a **preference** with a default. So: **electing the loop is
optional; once elected, every step is required; the one number in it is a stated default.** That
sentence belongs at the top of the shipped text. *Falsifier:* a stranger reading the skill's first
screen cannot tell how to decline it.

**H2 also carries one rule worth pressing on:** *"only during the final swing do we prioritize avoiding
regression."* The worry: swings 1–3 might break earlier fixes and nobody would look until the end. His
other rule answers it — *"Complete as much as possible of the QA process each time"* — because a whole
pass re-runs the whole checklist, a regression in swing 2 surfaces in swing 3's pass. The rule holds
**only while each swing runs the full plan.** *Falsifier:* a swing that re-checks only what it fixed.

**H3 — the two readers want different first steps, and the same finished thing.** "Best to work well
for others" wants the loop that counts: skill plus `sw1ng`. "Most likely tried by others" wants the
smallest thing a person can paste: twelve lines in CLAUDE.md and no install. gw7's RAD, beside this,
lays those out with parity. My reading as the place: build the counted version and ship the
paste-in text *inside* it, so the most-tried path is the first step onto the best path, not a fork away
from it.
