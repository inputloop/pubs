## What the list is

The list is ranked in four tiers, and in order of value within each tier. [MINE] The ranking puts user freedom and other people first, then the traction of the writing, then readiness to share. Effort is S (a sitting), M (a few sittings) or L (a stretch). **Needs host** means it needs Avalon (tools, Z/, the salt, git). **Safe without asking** is "yes" unless the item touches another person's material or custody, a file that is yours (signed or write-protected), or waits on one of your rulings. In those cases the column says why. Ids starting with F are rows in `writing/execute-brief/tracks/forks-inventory.tsv`. Ids starting with Q are bank rows.

Items marked "yes (…)" are safe only as they are shaped. A dissertation edit is a draft beside your text
(an ADD, not a MERGE). Decks get sidecars, never edits. MEMORY and ledgers get appended lines, never
rewrites. The bank gets a generated view, never a rewrite. A skill edit is followed by telling you, and
you re-sign.

freedom they are owed, for example a stranger is misled by a tool's output today. That item then belongs in
tier 1, and it moves.

(the second-user consent shape). **With Avalon:** 1–4 first, because they protect other people. Then 31
