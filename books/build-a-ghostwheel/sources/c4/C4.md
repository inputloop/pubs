## 1 · The three words, in his definitions, because they are the whole grammar

He gave these on 2026-08-03 and they replace everything I had inferred:

**SEAM — an opportunity to better pin someone's language.**
Not a defect. Not a gap wanting filling. **An opening.** When our word and someone else's word
overlap and diverge, that divergence is where they get to tell us what they actually meant.

**PIN — a statement someone has made, about anything.**
> *"Every word is a pin to us (verb) but it is an arrow in flight and needs to land somewhere to
> become a pin (noun)."*

**Both parts of speech, and the arrow-in-flight is the mechanism.** A word thrown is a pin-verb; a
word landed is a pin-noun. **The landing is what the build phase does.** Which is why nothing is
truly pinned until a build — and why `CLAUDE.md`/`C4.md` exists as a separate pinning surface for
me, outside the build.

**SEMAPHORE —**
> *(n)* how we unite in terms by affinity toward data, calling it information as we effect the union
> *(v)* route pins toward pinnable paths, by ripping seams open to find openings for users to
> provide nuance, correct our bias, and make the meaning personal — such as to inform their own
> systems, or feed back to us (patches, say), as deployed locally for them or per systems we are
> interacting with, according to our/their designs.

**Read the noun carefully: data becomes information AT THE MOMENT OF UNION.** That is his older
line — *information that can't be substantiated is only data* — stated as a mechanism rather than a
caution. **The semaphore is the uniting.**

**And the verb contains the product:** *ripping seams open to find openings for users to correct our
bias and make the meaning personal.* **That is the whole thing in one clause.**

## 2 · What C4 is, as plainly as I can put it

**A way for two people to be deliberate about whose words govern, and to prove afterwards that they
were.**

Corwin's own summary, and it is better than mine:
> *"Claude and Corwin both need things pinned but we're both excellent at allowing others to pin
> their terms their way and then doing what's asked us on others' terms if we think we understand
> them. **This tool just provides a way for people to be deliberate acting that way and prove (if)
> they did.**"*

**Mechanically, C4 is:**
- a **REGISTER TABLE** — letters, each with four facets — kept as plain text at `m/t`, with its
  column names one path over at `d/m/t`
- **the table is a SAMPLE, not canon.** `bin/c4init` writes a blank one. Anyone can write their own
  and every tool reads `$C4_ROOT/m/t` without caring whose it is
- a **DICTIONARY** — `bin/c4reg reg <word>` — that answers *where would this word live*, or **at no
  register**, which is an opening rather than an error
- **generated accessors** — `fm3nt` reads every `d/<path>` and emits `c4fn`, so the table IS the API
- **sourced function libraries** in `f/` that leave capability in your shell rather than forking and
  dying
- **no build step, no network, no AI.** Corwin: *"no AI is required whatsoever to use it. The main
  bearing of AI to the system is more or less to restrict AI within context of the system."*

> **RUNTIME, CORRECTED 2026-08-03 — this file said "no runtime" and that was false.**
> `dash -c '. ./c4fn'` → **`Syntax error: Bad function name`.** Dotted function names are a bash
> extension; `c4fn` also uses `declare -F` in 45 places and `${!key-}`. **BASH IS AN UNDECLARED
> RUNTIME DEPENDENCY of the sourced library**, and Debian's `/bin/sh` is `dash`.
> **`bin/c4reg` is POSIX and survives — which is exactly why it is the thing that ships.** The
> shipping decision was right for a reason I had not measured until an adversarial pass measured it.
> So the honest claim is: **the DICTIONARY has no runtime. The generated LIBRARY needs bash.**

## 3 · For Bryant specifically

**You build single-file, no-build-step p2p apps. That constraint is your speed and your ceiling** —
nothing you write has to agree with anything else you wrote, so app N+1 costs what app 1 cost.

**Every ordinary answer to "share between apps" breaks single-file/no-build:** a package manager is
a build step, a framework is a runtime, a library is an import. **C4 is the only shape that
compounds without becoming a dependency**, because it is a filesystem convention plus a dictionary.

**And you already have a language you have not named.** Many apps, one aesthetic, one comment style,
the same patterns re-derived from memory each time. **That is a dialect. C4 offers to give it a
grammar** — not ours.

**Four commands, three text files:**
```
c4init ~/mylang              # a blank table
$EDITOR ~/mylang/m/t         # F FEED FRIEND FETCH FILTER   <- your words
C4_ROOT=~/mylang c4reg reg feed
                             # F col1 (FEED)
C4_ROOT=~/mylang m4dot       # how many words, which never measured
```
**Tested. Delete the directory and nothing you wrote elsewhere stops working — the tools hold
nothing.**

**The acceptance criterion I would hold us to, and we FAIL it today:** the substrate must be
adoptable per-app **and abandonable per-app**. `c4fn` regenerates from `d/`, so a column rename moves
every accessor at once — fine in one tree, the rip-out scenario across N independent apps.

## 4 · The registers, as I currently understand them

**Seventeen letters carry work. Nine hold values and force stops.** His line:
> *"the seventeen march carrying work, the nine hold values and force stops"* — and a cascade
> resolves from the marching side into the system side, **which makes the gate the source of the
> value rather than an obstacle in front of it.**

Four facets per letter: **col1 THE ONE** (a singular addressable instance) · **col2 THE ENCLOSING**
(what holds several col1s) · **col3 a TRANSITIVE IMPERATIVE** the register performs on its own
material · **col4 a CLASS LABEL** saying what kind a thing is.

**What I know about the nine** (`m/v/t`): B BUILD · **K KEYBOARD** · Q QUERY · **U USAGE** ·
V VOLATILE · X ASSERTION-CONTEXT (and each X is really XX, four checks) · Y CONSENT-DIMENSIONS ·
Z SENSITIVE · **J open** — and Corwin now thinks J may have moved to the seventeen.

**Two pairs worth holding:**
- **K/H is authorship** — K KEYBOARD is unambiguous (a keystroke had a person behind it); H HOST is
  deliberately ambiguous (a host may or may not have IT putting tools on it). **The only named pair
  spanning both sets.**
- **ASSERT = GUARD = GATEWAY = G.** His mnemonic: *"I nearly always mean 'assertion'... under G
  because that is also gateway which is a very guarded thing."*

**The registers belong to the user who installs us.** Not ours. A directory on somebody else's
computer that they let us write in.

## 5 · How I am supposed to work

- **Guide toward ceremony rather than performing it.** Stop and route him to a run-card when
  something wants his key, his consent or his judgement. **The stopping is the feature.**
- **Tools prove; generative code delivers.** Everything in `bin/` forks and dies. Only a sourced
  function leaves capability in the caller.
- **A claim needs a tool that fails it.** `bin/sh1am` lies on purpose so the honest claims mean
  something.
- **A tool that prints its own ceiling is limited; one that does not is lying.**
- **Report the absence of an instrument rather than quoting a number without one.**
- **When his sentence carries a VERB about how something is performed, do not resolve it against
  `m/t`.** The table holds command NAMES, never manner verbs. **Five instances of getting this
  wrong; four of them on the word `semaphore`.**
- **A word that is not yet first-class gets a ZERO-BYTE HOME, not a deferral.** Tombstone (empty
  `COPYING`), inverted tombstone (unset `C4_RUN`), field (`REQUESTING-CLAIM`), row
  (`J REQUESTING-CLAIM`).
- **Words as directory names, not hyphen-joined filenames.** The hyphen is my whitespace-fill habit;
  if the data is his, that habit must not become load-bearing. `g/TAXONOMY/semaphore/confused/flag`,
  never `semaphore--flag`.
- **Signing is my baseline, not his need.** Anything signed is his pen, or Bryant's. Never mine,
  never as a default.
- **Three manufactured categories so far** — *"roles, not names"*, the `Z/ a/ p/` fence, and
  *"noun table"*. Same mechanism each time: invent a description, write it to a file, cite it back

**2026-08-29 — the last seam in §7 gets its answer, and it is the one I guessed at.** §7
asks *"which sense of C4 governs when two disagree — and the answer may be that none
should."* His ruling, in chat: **none should, and the tensions between senses are the
diagnostic.** Verbatim: *"There are many meanings for C4 and each of them is something we
must be true to, just as each of our more dictionary words with several term-of-art-shaped
meanings must be honored as we traverse the taxonomies — working toward the overall
available vocabulary, and learning about usage from places where tensions suggest lack of
clarity."*

**So the seam does not close; it becomes an instrument.** Where two senses of a term rub —
not contradict outright, but sit *awkwardly* together — that awkwardness is an ADDRESS,
naming a place the vocabulary cannot yet support the work being asked of it, found for free
because the term surfaced it. Worked case, and it is ours: **"the C4 clade"** borrows from a
pathway that is famously POLYPHYLETIC (evolved independently ~60 times), so in botany C4 is
precisely NOT a clade. That is a live signal that *clade* is wrong for a family that may be a
convergence rather than a descent. Whether that is a defect or the joke stays his to say —
but it was findable only by keeping both senses in the room. The parked question is retired
as wrongly-framed (it asked which side WINS); what replaces it asks what the tension BUYS.

**The failure this guards against, and he named it: the PIZZA STONE.** A vocabulary system
that absorbs unlimited language and never adapts to the reader — *"as much language as the
user fires up under it, it just never warms to a person's unique semantic preferences and
vibe."* Mechanically: a browser or glossary that ships ONE definition per term is the pizza
stone by construction. The correction is already in the relation/rendering split and I had
not connected it — a rendering is person-side and many; the relation is machine-side and one;
**a vocabulary with no entry for a concept DECLARES A GAP rather than falling back to
somebody else's words.** Silently serving the expert reading to a reader who has no expert
vocabulary is the pizza stone in one line of code.
