* Where they come from

The open shelf is the keeper's first public shelf (July 2026). Its sign-post cleared its pieces for
named readers first and "open internet when the keeper says wide"; his word for this move was given
on 2026-11-01. Only pieces he had signed, and that name no one outside the shop and no machine path,
came across. Some still carry the words "DRAFT" or "unsigned" from the day they were written: he
signed them afterwards as they stood, so the signature, not the label, says what each one is.
