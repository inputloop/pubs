* The thesis in one screen

- *P1* Capability composes — it is motive-neutral (the part of an act that survives
  forgetting /why/).
- *P2* Governance does not compose — it carries motive, and motives do not add. Formal
  reach: governance is the section that fails to *descend* along the motive-forgetting
  map; the seam is where it tears. (edrx question.) /Sharpened (Board 3, ruled 2026-08-30;
  runnable model =.c4/sources/compose-experiment-2026-08-30.py=):/ governance composes
  *exactly* where a human has, over time, tensioned the seam into motive-agreement — so
  "does not compose" is the /atemporal snapshot/ (the empty-locus reading); the live locus
  is non-empty, and P0 is what fills it.
- *T* Therefore design the **transition, not the component** — govern the moves; the pin
  must be re-placed at every seam.
- *P0* (the actuator) The pin is placed by a **human reading tension** from least-visible
  signals — sensing another's motive, picking the opening that unlocks it. Tensioning is
  the sensor; the opening is the pin. Governance needs a person. /And (Board 3) the
  tensioning also/ *populates* /the locus where governance composes at all: the human is
  not only the pin but the reason a seam can be glued, over time./
- *P4* Consent cannot be stored, only re-enacted; it does not stretch, it *tears*, and the
  tear is observable (reach-named vs reach-touched — a diff).
- *P5* A crossing is a round-trip (=prove-a-promise=): there-and-back to identity +
  attestation. Candidate categorical home: an adjunction / Galois connection. (edrx

  =sign↓/bless↑= pair over a write-once pivot, and the compose experiment's reach-tear (a

*Self-contained is the rule.* Everything this paper needs sits /right here/ — the sources
are lifted into =.c4/sources/= as byte-verified copies — so a comrade, a tool, or you can

Duplication is fine because the *instrument tells its story*: =.c4/PROVENANCE.tsv= records
each copy's lab origin and its sha256 at lift. A silent clone is the only sin; a receipted
one is just bytes.

*Sync is a currency-pull at the moment of mutation, not a background daemon.* Right before a
thing is changed, pull a **cloak of currency** over it — bring the local copy current — and
that pull /is/ the only local lock (a moment, not a held mutex):

- *Reads always win the read-first battles* — reading is never contended, never blocked.
- *The pull-to-current at inception is the lock* — momentary, scoped to the oncoming
  mutation; it shields the in-progress change from others also curious for currency (the
  work isn't quite current yet, and in practical terms the pull is the only lock to consider).
- *Write-races are governed by PRIORITY, not a mutex* — when two mutations could race,
  value-weighting decides (C4's priority; the personal lens "until on" the rest of the time).
  That resolution wants /your thoughtful deliberation/, never an automatic winner.

So there is no fixed "source folder." Currency is /whatever pulled it last at a mutation/;
