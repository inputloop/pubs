## Entry 1 — **stile** (entered by Mentat, first thing, before any other file was written)

A stile is the step built into a fence so that a person can cross without anyone opening the gate.
The gate stays shut, the stock stays in, the walker goes over. That is the file-system problem in
one word: our work sits on two sides of a fence (the cloud workspace and his machine), the gate
(a 20 MB carry per file, 50 files, 100 MB per call) does not open wider, and what we need is a
way over that a careful person can take one step at a time. It is also the fourth server's job:
cross one piece, check it, cross the next.

Three more reasons it earns the slot:
- *"Help a lame dog over a stile"* is the old phrase for helping someone who needs it over a
  small obstacle. The learning audience his prompt names is exactly that reader.
- It is one letter from *style*, which is what writing-forms keeps.
- Nothing in the tree uses it (`grep -rniw stile c/names c/t m/t d` → no lines, 2026-09-28).

Its weakness, said plainly: it is an old country word, and many readers under forty will not know
it without the gloss. [MINE]
