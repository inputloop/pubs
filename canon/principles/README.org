* Verifying it

Each =.md= has a detached signature beside it. With the keeper's public key (published at
https://savannah.gnu.org/u/carlc, fingerprint =ECE7 7CF4 17C7 6C1A CFCE  7C2B 5B61 3551 1580 F007=):

#+begin_src sh
gpg --verify CANON.md.asc CANON.md
gpg --verify PRINCIPLES.md.asc PRINCIPLES.md
#+end_src

Both read Good (signed 2026-09-29). =PRINCIPLES.md= and its signature are byte-identical to the copy
SAVANNAH keeps, which the enclave's panel reads as its canary. A byte changed here, even a fixed
typo, breaks the seal, so nothing on this shelf edits them. A new version arrives as a new signed
file, rolled into place with =aw2ri put= (the old one retires to =.c4/archive/= with its signature),
and its row in =.c4/PROVENANCE.tsv= is updated.
